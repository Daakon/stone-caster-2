Worlds written by people

Every story starts from handcrafted places, characters and lore. The narrator adapts them to you; it never invents the world from nothing.

Rules you can see

Each story picks its rulesets: five pillars, stamina magic, survival, relationships. Rolls and changes show up as plain receipts under every turn.

People who remember

Tap any name to see what you know about them, where you learned it, and what is still unknown. Nothing you haven't discovered is shown.

How a turn plays

Say what you do. The story answers.

Choose Do, Say or Look, write a line, and send. You'll see the scene read, your action resolved and the next beat written, then choices to continue with. A turn costs a few Stones; every account starts with a grant.
