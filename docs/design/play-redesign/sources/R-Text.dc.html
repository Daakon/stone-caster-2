Text as the interfaceEvery line of the story is typed, and every noun is a doorThe Narrator does not send a paragraph. It sends typed blocks whose names, places and objects resolve to real entities. That lets the page read like a good RPG script: speakers, inner voices, discoveries and choices, all still prose.
